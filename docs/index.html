Arisan — Rotating savings circles on Tari Ootle

⚡ Walletless demo — simulated in your browser, no wallet needed

An arisan is a rotating savings circle: every member contributes a fixed amount each round, and each round one member — in join order — takes the whole pot. This page runs the exact rules of the Arisan template as a simulation, so you can try a full circle, watch a member default, and see how the leftover pot is shared — without spending anything.

How it works

  1. Join a circle by paying one contribution up front — that is your round-1 payment. The circle starts automatically once the last seat is taken.
  2. Contribute the fixed amount every round. Once everyone in good standing has paid (or the round deadline passes), anyone can settle the round and the recipient takes the whole pot.
  3. Miss a deadline and you default: you lose your payout turn, the pot rolls over to the next round, and your past contributions stay in the pot.
  4. When the circle completes, any leftover pot is shared equally by members in good standing — or by everyone if all defaulted, so funds are never stuck.

This page simulates the template logic in your browser. The real template is src/lib.rs (Rust, #[template]), tested with the Tari engine test harness. Every demo account starts with 1,000 simulated TARI.
